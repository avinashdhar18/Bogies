Position Description  ·  Bogie's at Depot Square  ·  Hampton, NH
Front of House
Manager
Leadership & Operations
✦

This is a management position for the Bogie's at Depot Square business. Position requires management skills including leading by example, preparing for the venue's daily operations, and planning for upcoming venue events. Communicating with staff and kitchen is vital for success. Taking initiative, problem solving, and making sound business decisions when an immediate decision is required. Maintaining a front of house venue that is clean and organized with a place for all products and front of house items. Always presenting the business in a positive light and keeping personal issues outside the venue.

§ 01
Daily Staffing & Operations
  • Manage the proper level of labor needed to carry out daily operations. Ensure the proper staff member is in place to perform.
  • Understand specials of the day including soups, entrées, and desserts. Staff should know what the kitchen is trying to sell and make proper suggestions to guests.
  • Greet guests ASAP when entering the venue. The touch will reduce walk-ups and leave people.
  • Serve drinks and beverages to patrons in a timely manner.
  • Put food orders in and run food to guests in a timely and efficient manner.
  • Maintain a high standard of presentation of food and drink products — proper plate setups including sauces, condiments, napkins, and silverware.
  • Efficiently keep tables clear of empty baskets and wipe down tables after patrons' departure.
  • Always maintain clean floors during the day and a 100% sweeper at end of day with mopping if necessary.
  • Oversee daily cleaning and ongoing cleaning maintenance programs for the front of house including the event room.
  • End of day trash removal to outside dumpsters.
  • Oversee daily remit and tip distribution process. Communicate with owners with a reconciliation of changes to remit on close-out report.
§ 02
Order of Product Responsibility
  • Order and pick up liquor for the venue. Communicate to owners of all issues including drink specials that require unique and expensive liquor purchases.
  • Maintain and order beer products by working with distributors — ensure products are not dated, kegs are refrigerated, empty kegs are returned weekly, and a stock beer coaster is maintained.
  • Walk-in cooler beer is organized and properly rotated. Work with kitchen staff on the organization of product in this cooler.
  • All bar-related products (juices, sauces, fruits, milks, creams, etc.) need to be communicated timely — text or photo of products needed — to owners so an order can be made.
§ 03
Hiring, Scheduling, Discipline & Termination
  • Responsible for the hiring of bartenders, servers, barbacks, and runners. Work within a labor dollar budget that considers business effectiveness, sales volume, and adequate compensation.
  • Prepare a weekly schedule based on events of the week. Owners will supply an updated schedule — use the app Time Tree.
  • Communicate with each staff member what their position responsibilities are and follow up when those responsibilities are not met. Discipline, including termination if necessary, is required when tasks are not completed to business satisfaction.
  • Drinking on the job is a ground for immediate termination.
§ 04
Venue Presentation
  • Maintain the front of house set-up and design, and in coordination with the owners make changes that are consistent with the venue's concept.
